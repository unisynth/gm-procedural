# assets/gm-plus — provenance

This directory holds the structure of the Windows `gm.dls` bank (instrument and region layout, articulation, sample
parameters, no audio), the per-wave target map, and synthesis parameters fitted against the gmplus soundfont. gmplus
is a fan soundfont built from Roland Sound Canvas samples (Release 1, by Distortion Dog). The parameters describe
synthesis models, not sample data. Neither `gm.dls` nor `gmplus.sf2` is committed: they are read from `GM_DLS` and
`GMPLUS_SF2` on the developer's machine. Change: add-gmplus-bank.
