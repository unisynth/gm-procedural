gm-procedural — listening pages

Procedural General MIDI bank: every wave of the Windows gm.dls (and of the gmplus soundfont) rebuilt from a small set of synthesis parameters. These pages put the original next to the generated sound. Code, algorithms and docs: github.com/unisynth/gm-procedural.

Songs and sounds

  • Four-way song A/B — whole GM songs through gm.dls, gmplus, gen-gm, gen-plus (keys 1–4 switch bank)
  • All 495 sounds — every wave, four versions, gmfit scores

Fitting rounds

  • Pilot A/B — kick, snare, hat, bass, strings, piano (round 4)
  • GM drums, pass 4
  • Named drums — hearing model
  • Score vs hearing — where the score and the ear model disagree
  • Cymbal loops
  • Karplus-Strong attacks
  • Stream and vibraslap
  • Water rings

Sources: MIDI — John Novak, Grand MS-DOS gaming General MIDI showdown (archive.org) and The Very Best of GUS MIDI (archive.org BESTMID6). Banks — Windows gm.dls (© Microsoft / Roland, read from a local copy, never published) and gmplus (fan soundfont from Roland Sound Canvas samples, Release 1, by Distortion Dog). All audio is rendered by gm-procedural, for comparison only.
